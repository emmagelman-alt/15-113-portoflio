            Where do I find this link?
            1. Open Outlook on the web and go to Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars.
            2. Under Publish a calendar, pick your calendar and Can view all details, then click Publish.
            3. Copy the ICS link (it ends in .ics) and paste it here.

            Anyone with that link can see your calendar, so it's kept on the dashboard server and only your own meetings are shown to you. Unpublish it in Outlook to revoke it.
